Pentagon Chain — $PC top-up

Pentagon Chain is a gamified ecosystem. $PC is its prepaid utility credit — the same idea as an iTunes balance, Roblox Robux, or Twitch Bits: an access/gas credit you use inside the ecosystem.

Notice: following a recent, unintended exploit, the previous bridge is fully locked. This is the new, secured one-way top-up path — a 2-of-2 signed, on-chain, trustless flow with open visibility to everyone's status.

In short: this is a one-way top-up. Ethereum $PC you send is locked permanently and you receive a non-transferable in-ecosystem credit with no cash value and no route back out. Please read the full terms below — scroll to the end to enable the button.

  • One-way only — you move $PC into the ecosystem; there is no user route back out to Ethereum.
  • The Ethereum $PC you top up is locked permanently; you receive an in-ecosystem credit.
  • Rewards for effort & progress (leaderboards, VIP tiers) are funded separately, on-chain and transparent.

  • Listed on any major exchange (CEX/DEX), or guaranteed ever to be
