    🛡️ Payout guards Pentagon Chain

    A refilling bucket bounds how fast the programme can ever pay out, so a compromised signer pair can't drain the pool. Sized for the programme's current size — step it up as commitments grow.

    GuardNowWhat it means
    Refill rate–max sustained payout, forever
    Burst cap–most that can leave in one moment
    Available now–currently banked in the bucket
    Daily ceiling–coarse second bound
    Per-position cap–largest single mirrored stake

    Step up the guard

    Two rules: the refill rate must exceed the whole programme's daily accrual, and the burst cap must never sit below the largest legitimate single claim (a 100 $PC position left unclaimed for two years is 36 $PC) or a patient staker gets stuck.
